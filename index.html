01 / The essentials

What are non-GamStop casinos?

Start with the distinction between a search phrase, a self-exclusion service and an operating licence.

People researching casinos not on GamStop UK need more than a list of brand names. The phrase describes an absence from a self-exclusion network; it does not certify a casino’s reliability, establish permission to serve British customers or demonstrate that withdrawals will be paid.

GAMSTOP states that all online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain must be part of its service. That makes the licensing question central to any comparison of non-GamStop casinos—not a footnote beneath a promotional offer. [2]

Overseas licensing is not UK authorisation

The Gambling Commission says that commercial operators need its relevant operating licence to provide gambling to consumers in Great Britain, regardless of where the business is based. A licence issued in another country does not provide that authorisation. Great Britain here means England, Scotland and Wales; the wording should not be silently extended to every jurisdiction. [1]

For this guide, “best” is a question to investigate, not a conclusion already reached. A site’s appearance, its use of pounds or an English-language interface would not answer the underlying questions. Neither would a screenshot of a licence logo without a matching, current register entry.

The first check: identify the legal company, the exact website domain, the regulator and the licence status. The Gambling Commission’s public register is the official starting point for checking a claimed British licence. [3]
